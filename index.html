Companion tools for the M/EEG Conceptual Guide

Recording, Mapping and Analyzing Brain Signals and Networks: A Conceptual Guide to Methods and Applications of M/EEG in Psychology and Neuroscience, by Evangelos Paraskevopoulos.

For readers and students

Ask a question about M/EEG methods and get a short answer, with a pointer to the chapter and section of the book where you can read more.

Open the tutor

For lecturers

Generate exam questions based on the book, and get a proposed grade with a short rationale for a student answer. You always decide the final grade.

You will need the access code that comes with the lecturer material.

Open the lecturer tools

Good to know

  • Answers are generated by AI and can contain errors. Please check anything important against the book.
  • The tutor gives short answers on purpose. It is a study aid and not a substitute for reading the book, and it is meant for personal study, not for systematically copying the book's content.
  • In the lecturer tools, paste only the text of an answer. Never include names, student numbers or anything else that could identify a person. A proposed grade is only a suggestion.
  • The tools are free, need no account, and are provided as they are. They may change, or be limited by daily usage caps.

See the privacy notice for what happens to the text you type.
